Rules-accurate simulation

Four of your saved decks play full Commander games on the phase-rs rules engine (real stack, priority, combat, layers and triggers). Every seat is played by the engine's AI. Games that stall or hit the action cap are reported, never counted as wins.

Checking the engine…
A ticked seat is played by the model instead of phase's built-in AI. The model only chooses among the engine's own legal options (it cannot invent a move), is asked at most 300 questions per run, and anything it gets wrong or is too slow for is played by the built-in AI. Model seats take much longer than built-in AI seats.
